Dental Treatments We Offer in Antalya, Turkey

With our belief in the importance of correct diagnosis, planning and treatment of oral and dental health, we continue our work with the happiness and pleasure we get from our profession and our dear patients in our clinic.

DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as artificial tooth roots to support replacement teeth, restoring functionality, aesthetics, and preserving bone health for long-term oral well-being.

Read More about Dental Implants

ALL ON 4

The All-on-4 treatment is a comprehensive solution for replacing a full arch of missing teeth using four strategically placed implants, providing stability, function, and aesthetics for a confident smile.

Read More about All-on-4

ALL ON 6

The All-on-6 treatment involves the placement of six implants to support a full arch of teeth, providing enhanced stability, durability, and a natural-looking smile for improved chewing and confidence.

Read More about All-on-6

HOLLYWOOD SMILE DESIGN

A Hollywood smile makeover is a comprehensive cosmetic dental treatment that aims to enhance the appearance of a person's smile. It typically involves procedures like teeth whitening, dental veneers, crowns, orthodontic treatments, gum contouring, and dental implants. The goal is to create a flawless and radiant smile.

Read More about Hollywood Smile

ZIRCONIUM CROWNS

Zirconium crowns are dental restorations made from zirconium oxide ceramic, known for their exceptional strength, durability, and lifelike aesthetics. They offer natural-looking and highly resistant solutions for restoring damaged or discolored teeth.

Read More about Zirconium Crowns

E-Max

E-Max is a high-quality, all-ceramic dental material known for its exceptional aesthetics, strength, and durability. It is commonly used for veneers, crowns, and other restorations to achieve natural-looking and long-lasting results.

Read More about E-Max
