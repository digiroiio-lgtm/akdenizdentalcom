What are All-on-4 dental implants?

For patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, the All-on- 4 dental implant approach marks a revolution. This creative surgery supports a whole arch of artificial teeth with only four titanium implants. We strategically position these implants to improve stability and minimize the need for additional procedures like bone grafting. So, the outcome is a practical, natural-looking set of teeth that feel just like your own. (Click for detailed information)

Who is suitable for All-on-4 dental implants?

  • Those who have lost all jaw teeth
  • Those who are dissatisfied with their dentures
  • Those who have chewing and speech problems due to tooth loss
  • Those whose gums and bone structure are suitable for implant placement

How much does All-on-4 cost in the UK vs Turkey?

Countries charge different prices for All-on-4 dental implants. UK vs. Turkey:

In the United Kingdom, the cost of All-on-4 dental implants ranges from £12,000 to £20,000 per arch. The high price is attributable to UK overhead and living expenses.

In Turkey, the cost of All-on-4 dental implants ranges from £3,000 to £6,000 per arch. Lower operating costs and a favourable exchange rate reduce the price. However, many Turkish clinics provide top-notch facilities and expert dentists, despite reduced pricing.

The All-on-4 Procedure: What to Expect

Initial Consultation: Your journey begins with a thorough consultation. Your dentist will examine your teeth, take digital and 3D scans, and discuss treatment.

Treatment Planning: Following the decision to proceed, we prepare a comprehensive treatment plan. This strategy uses digital and 3D scans to analyse the jawbone and build artificial teeth.

Implant Placement: On the day of treatment, the dentist will insert four titanium implants into your jawbone. To avoid discomfort, you'll get local anaesthesia or sedative. After attaching temporary prosthetic teeth, you leave the clinic with a new grin.

Healing Period: After a few months, the implants will merge with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation for your permanent teeth. Your dentist will prescribe a particular care routine.

Final Prosthetics: Once the implants have completely fused, you will return to the clinic to receive your custom-made permanent teeth. These will match your natural teeth's colour, shape, and size for a seamless effect.
