For most people with one or a few missing teeth and enough healthy bone, dental implants are the best option in Antalya, Turkey, because they leave the neighbouring teeth untouched and keep the jawbone working. But the position of those neighbouring teeth, bone volume, general health and the number of trips someone can make may point to a bridge instead.

Choosing a missing teeth treatment in Antalya: which option fits your case?

Start with the gap, not the price list. Dentists in Antalya usually look at three things first: how many teeth are missing, what the neighbouring teeth look like and how much bone is left.

Your situationUsually considered first
One missing tooth, healthy neighbours, enough boneSingle implant
One missing tooth, neighbours already need crownsBridge or implant
One missing tooth, neighbours have tilted into the gapCloser assessment needed
Several missing teeth in a rowImplant-supported bridge
Most or all teeth missing in a jawAll-on-4 or All-on-6
Not enough boneBone graft, or a bridge

A table can’t examine anyone, of course. Treat it as a starting point.

Dental implants for missing teeth in Antalya, Turkey

An implant is a small titanium post that sits in the jawbone. It acts as a replacement root and carries the crown.

Many patients lean towards implants for two reasons. The teeth next to the gap stay untouched, and chewing forces keep reaching the bone (which tends to shrink once a root is gone).

When is an implant not the right choice?

Sometimes the problem is general health. Uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking and untreated gum disease can all raise the risk of implant failure, so these need attention before surgery.

And sometimes the problem is the neighbouring teeth.

When a tooth has been missing for many years, the teeth beside it can drift, tilt and become crowded. The gap slowly closes, and what’s left may be too small for an implant. Position matters even when an implant does fit. Because the neighbouring teeth have moved, a triangular space can remain between the implant crown and the teeth next to it.

Food collects in that space. Over time, this makes cleaning the area very important, because poor cleaning can shorten the life of the implant.

So a patient in this situation has two realistic paths:

  • Keep the implant and clean very thoroughly, every day, to protect it.
  • Choose a dental bridge instead, if that routine doesn’t seem realistic.

In some cases, orthodontic treatment can also move the teeth and reopen the space before an implant is placed. An online examination can show which path makes sense.

Traditional dental bridges in Antalya: when do they make sense?

A traditional bridge closes the gap with a false tooth, anchored by crowns on the teeth at each side. No surgery. No long wait for healing.

The catch: those neighbouring teeth have to be reduced, and that can’t be undone. If they’re healthy and untouched, that’s a real cost. If they’re heavily filled or already need crowns, a bridge starts to look far more sensible.

A bridge also suits patients who don’t want surgery, or who have too little bone and would rather avoid a graft. It doesn’t replace the root, though, so the bone under the false tooth can still shrink. And the area beneath it needs daily cleaning with floss threaders or small interdental brushes.

Implant-supported bridges for several missing teeth in Turkey

Three teeth missing in a row doesn’t always mean three implants. Two implants can often carry a bridge across the whole space, which means less surgery and no reduction of natural teeth.

Whether that works depends on where the gap is, the bite and the bone available.

All teeth missing? Full-arch options in Antalya

When most or all teeth in a jaw are missing, individual replacements stop making sense. Full-arch treatment places a fixed set of teeth on a small number of implants: four with All-on-4, six with All-on-6. Those coming from a removable denture can read more about getting implants after dentures.

What happens if missing teeth are not replaced?

Not much at first, which is exactly why people wait.

Then the changes build slowly. Bone in the empty area shrinks. Neighbouring teeth drift into the gap, and the tooth opposite can move out of position because it has nothing to bite against. Chewing shifts to the other side.

None of this means an emergency. It does mean the options can narrow with time, and a gap left for years may need more work than a recent one.

How many trips to Antalya does each treatment take?

Timelines vary from patient to patient, but this is the typical pattern:

  • Traditional bridge: usually one trip.
  • Single implant or implant-supported bridge: usually two visits, with several months of healing between them.
  • All-on-4 or All-on-6: usually two visits, with temporary teeth during healing depending on the patient.
  • With a bone graft or sinus lift: healing can take longer, so the second visit may move further out.

How much does it cost to replace missing teeth in Turkey?

Prices listed by clinics in Turkey start from about £400 for a single implant with a crown and about £450 for a three-unit bridge. These are general market starting points, not a quote.

The final figure depends on the implant brand, crown material, number of teeth and any extra work such as extractions or grafting. So ask what’s included, in writing. Abutment? Crown? Scans? Temporary teeth? A low headline price can leave out some of these.

How do dentists in Antalya choose the right treatment?

They examine first and recommend second.

A proper assessment covers the teeth and gums, the bite, medical history, smoking habits and bone, usually with 3D imaging. It also includes the question patients often skip: what can someone realistically maintain at home? An implant that needs meticulous cleaning is a poor fit for someone who won’t do it. A good plan accounts for that.

Ask why one option was recommended over the others. A clear answer should mention your own teeth, not just the treatment.
