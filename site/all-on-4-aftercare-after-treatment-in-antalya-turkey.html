All on 4 aftercare begins before you leave Antalya and recovery is not identical for everyone. Bone grafting, general health and the number of arches treated can all affect the healing period. Mild discomfort, swelling and bruising are common during the early stage. These symptoms should gradually settle rather than become more severe.

Your All on 4 recovery timeline at a glance

This is a general guide. Your personal All on 4 recovery timeline may be different.

How should you care for All on 4 implants at home?

Good home care protects the healing gum tissue and reduces unnecessary pressure on the implants. It does not mean cleaning the surgical area aggressively. During the early stage, a gentle and consistent routine is usually more appropriate.

Take prescribed medicines exactly as directed. Do not start antibiotics, antiseptic mouthwash or stronger pain medication without professional advice. If you have questions after leaving Turkey, contact the treating clinic before changing the instructions yourself.

The first 24 hours after all on 4 in Antalya

Avoid repeatedly spitting or rinsing forcefully, as this can disturb the blood clot around a surgical site.

Rest with your head slightly raised. Place a cold pack against the outside of the face if your clinical team has advised it. Wrap the pack in a clean cloth rather than putting ice directly on the skin.

For the first 24 hours:

  • Do not touch the surgical areas with your fingers or tongue.
  • Avoid hot food and hot drinks.
  • Do not use a straw.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Take only the medicines approved by your dentist.
  • Do not smoke or vape.
  • Do not drink alcohol.

Your recovery timeline

  • First 24 hours: Mild swelling and tenderness.
  • Days 2–3: Swelling may be more noticeable.
  • First week: Discomfort starts to ease.
  • Weeks 2–6: Gums continue to settle.
  • First 4–6 months: Implants integrate with the jawbone.

Key aftercare tips

  • Rest: Keep your head slightly raised.
  • Take your medication: As prescribed.
  • Eat soft foods: Cut food into small pieces.
  • Avoid smoking and vaping: They can slow healing.
  • No alcohol: At least 24 hours and while on medication.
  • Keep it clean: Use a soft toothbrush and gentle rinses.

The first week of all on 4 post-operative care

Swelling often becomes more noticeable during the first two or three days. It should then begin to reduce. Your mouth still needs to be kept clean, but the surgical sites require care.

In the early days, you can gargle with salt water to maintain good oral hygiene. Gargling too frequently will not speed up recovery. On the contrary, it may irritate the tissues. The painkillers and antibiotics prescribed by the dentist should be taken at regular intervals and for the specified duration.

All on 4 healing process during the first 4-6 months

All on 4 surgical sites may appear healed on the outside before the implants have fully integrated with the jawbone. Depending on the treatment plan and individual healing, this process may take several months and is known as osseointegration.

The temporary bridge allows you to leave the clinic with fixed teeth, but it does not mean that the implants are ready for unrestricted chewing. Excessive pressure during this stage may disturb healing. Hard foods should therefore remain off the menu until the clinical team confirms that implants and bridge can manage them.

All on 4 temporary bridge care at home-Antalya

A temporary All on 4 bridge Antalya is designed for the healing period. It is fixed in place, so it should not be removed at home. Although it allows you to speak and smile, it is not as strong as the planned final bridge.

Avoid testing it with hard or sticky food. Ice, nuts, hard crusts, boiled sweets and chewy confectionery can place concentrated force on the temporary teeth. Cutting food into small pieces also makes eating more manageable.

Smoking, vaping and all on 4 healing timeline

Smoking impairs healing around dental implants. It reduces blood supply to the tissues and is associated with a higher risk of implant complications. Vaping is not a harmless alternative during recovery. Nicotine still affects blood flow, while suction and heat may irritate recently treated areas.

Stopping before surgery and remaining smoke-free throughout healing gives the tissues a better environment in which to recover.

Frequently asked questions about all on 4 aftercare

How long does all on 4 recovery take?

Initial discomfort and swelling often improve during the first week or two. However, integration between All on 4 implants and jawbone usually continues for several months. Many final bridges are considered after around 4-6 months.

Why no dairy after dental implants?

Avoiding dairy products is not a universal rule after dental implant surgery. Milk, yoghurt and other dairy foods do not automatically interfere with implant healing. Your dentist may still advise you to avoid particular products for a short period. This could relate to nausea after sedation, individual dietary needs or instructions connected with medication. Follow the advice given for your treatment rather than applying a general “no dairy” rule.

Is all on 4 dental implant care difficult?

All on 4 dental implant care is manageable once you understand where plaque collects. The bridge does not develop decay, but plaque can still build up where it meets the gums and around the implant connections.

You can carry out your routine dental care using a soft-bristled toothbrush and an interdental brush. The same dental care routine is followed as with natural teeth, and cleaning and maintaining All-on-4 in Antalya is not difficult.

How long should you wait to drink alcohol after dental implants?

Alcohol should be avoided for at least the first 24 hours after implant treatment. Your dentist may recommend a longer period after extensive surgery such as full-arch implant placement.

Alcohol can also interact with certain antibiotics and pain medicines. Do not drink while taking medication unless a dentist, doctor or pharmacist has confirmed that it is safe. Waiting until bleeding has stopped, swelling is settling and medication is no longer required is the more cautious approach.
